# Font licences

Montserrat and Source Sans 3 are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Original projects and licence text:
- Montserrat: https://github.com/JulietaUla/Montserrat
- Source Sans: https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-sans
- Google Fonts Montserrat licence: https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/montserrat/OFL.txt
- Google Fonts Source Sans 3 licence: https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/sourcesans3/OFL.txt

Font files were retrieved from Google Fonts and subset to Latin characters and punctuation, then compressed to WOFF for local web serving. The glyph artwork is unchanged. The licence texts are included alongside the files in public/assets.
