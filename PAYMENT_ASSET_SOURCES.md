# Payment logo sources

Eight payment logos match the brands and order in the user-provided reference. These are display assets; this change does not implement a checkout or payment processor integration.

- Apple Pay, Google Pay, Stripe, Visa and mada: https://github.com/activemerchant/payment_icons/tree/master/app/assets/images/payment_icons
- Mastercard reference badge: https://github.com/activemerchant/payment_icons/blob/b17637c407a7bb73dcfdddae2214bee05a2434b2/app/assets/images/payment_icons/master.svg
- Tabby reference badge: https://github.com/activemerchant/payment_icons/blob/7ea1881867b07f152b5dc0c88a7c952280eb0217/app/assets/images/payment_icons/tabby.svg
- Tamara gradient badge: official English Wordmark Gradient.svg from https://cdn.tamara.co/merchant_docs/Tamara__Brand__Assets_v2.zip

Original SVG paths are retained. Unsupported display-p3 fill declarations are removed in favour of the supplied sRGB fallback colours. Brand marks belong to their respective owners.
